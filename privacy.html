Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how Tahat Just Math handles information when you use this educational website. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and currently has no user accounts, analytics service, advertising, or server-side quiz submission.

Information entered into quizzes

Quiz answers are processed in your browser to provide practice and scoring. If you choose Email My Result, the site opens your email application with the assessment name and score in a draft addressed to contact@tahatjustmath.com. The site does not receive the draft or submit it to a Tahat Just Math server. You decide whether to send it, and your email provider handles any message you send under its own privacy terms.

Contact email

If you email contact@tahatjustmath.com, Cloudflare Email Routing forwards your message to the designated Outlook inbox. Cloudflare and Microsoft may process the message under their own privacy terms. The website itself does not store contact emails. Avoid sending student records or other sensitive information.

Hosting and external services

GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for information about GitHub's data practices.

When a page needs mathematical notation, it may load MathJax from jsDelivr. This causes a request to that service; jsDelivr may receive standard connection information such as your IP address. See jsDelivr's privacy policy. Other external links leave this site and are governed by the linked service's policies.

The website itself does not currently use cookies, local storage, or analytics to track visitors.

Advertising and cookies

No advertisements are currently served on this website. If advertising is added, this policy will be updated before ads are enabled to identify the ad providers and explain their data practices.

If Google advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's previous visits to this site or other sites. Visitors can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings and can learn about additional industry opt-outs at aboutads.info. Any other ad vendors used will be identified here with links to their privacy and opt-out information.

When serving personalized ads to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google requires publishers to use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. No advertising or consent platform is active on this site today.

Audience

This educational site is aimed primarily at students aged 13 and older. It does not ask users to create accounts or provide personal information to access lessons and practice. No age verification is performed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site's features or third-party services change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

For website feedback, see the Contact page.
