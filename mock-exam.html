Mock Exam

Practise under real exam conditions. The mock exam is timed and monitored by the TestPortal proctoring platform, so it feels like a real test: no pausing, no going back to notes, and your behaviour during the test is recorded for your instructor.

Time allowed40 minutes
Questions20 (random each attempt)
AttemptsUp to 10

Important test instructions

  • Number of questions: the test has 20 questions.
  • Question randomization: questions are selected randomly for each new attempt.
  • Time limit: 2 minutes (02:00) per question, 40 minutes in total. When the 2 minutes expire, the system automatically moves to the next question and the score for the unsubmitted question is forfeited.
  • Allowed attempts: you may take the test up to 10 times.
  • Calculators:
    • Allowed: a personal handheld scientific calculator (for example, a Casio model).
    • Strictly prohibited: any on-screen, device or in-browser calculator, and opening external apps. Doing so takes focus away from the test and triggers an automatic block.
  • Security and monitoring:
    • Do not leave the test page, switch tabs, minimize the window, or open other applications or windows.
    • Leaving or unfocusing the test page more than 3 times, for any reason, results in an automatic block and cancellation of your test session.
  • Use a laptop or desktop with a stable internet connection, and allow full screen and the camera or microphone if the test asks.
  • Do not copy, paste, take screenshots or refresh the page. Enter your real name and student ID when asked.
Academic integrity: Cheating, impersonation and leaving the exam window can lead to a zero or other penalties set by your instructor.

How to prepare

Review the chapter lessons and guided examples, finish the practice quiz in the chapter, and rehearse with your calculator in the Calculator tips tab. Then return here when you are ready.
